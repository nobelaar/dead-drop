## Summary

Tomás is a junior software developer, a bit younger than [Nicolás Ferreyra](nicolas-ferreyra.md). Nico helped him get his first tech job — an opportunity that mattered — but Tomás is not Nico’s protégé. He has his own aspirations and is building a life that does not depend only on who opened the first door.

## Personality

Sharp, social, and more impulsive than he thinks. Curious about learning; can get very excited about new tech, still short on experience to tell a real opportunity from something that only *looks* good.

Quick humor; enjoys casual talk. Sometimes uses jokes to hide insecurity or the feeling that others have clearer life plans.

Not incompetent and not a child the others must babysit. Early career, lots still to learn.

Can get proud when treated as less capable — especially knowing part of his first break came through Nico.

---

## Background

- Junior software developer (not red team / not Nico’s skill clone).
- First tech job likely via Nico recommending him for something he would not have landed alone yet.
- Rents a small apartment; watches expenses carefully. Not destitute; no serious financial cushion.
- First job let him start independence and stop feeling his future depended only on catching a break.
- Life project not fully defined — wants to move forward without always knowing where.

---

## Goals

- Economic independence and a career built on merit (not only “the kid Nico got a job for”).
- Progress, earn better, eventually help others the way someone helped him.
- Under crisis: path he had started is under threat, not only safety.
