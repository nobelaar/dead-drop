## Summary

Santiago (“Santi”) is an AI specialist and musician, several years older than [Nicolás Ferreyra](nicolas-ferreyra.md). He is married to Camila; together they are building a house that eats savings and forces constant practical decisions.

He is a competent professional who enjoys his work — not a tech guru or misunderstood genius — with normal limits: a job, expenses, arriving home tired.

## Personality

Calm, curious, and quite creative. Easy to get excited by new ideas — especially tech or music — and can scatter across projects, doing several things at once.

Relaxed humor, sometimes ironic. Does not need to be the center of attention to enjoy a gathering. Can go from a complex technical discussion to **playing piano** or talking complete nonsense within minutes.

**Main contradiction:** he can pour huge time into exploring an idea he loves while postponing practical tasks he knows he should finish.

Not a flattened label (married friend / musician / AI guy). Own wants, contradictions, problem-solving style.

---

## Background

- AI specialist — exact domain still flexible; not a universal systems/comms/infrastructure expert.
- Musician: plays **piano** and composes on his own. Music is an important part of identity; not necessarily his main income.
- Building a house with Camila (not a professional builder). Project consumes much of their savings: what to buy first, what they can DIY, what to defer.
- Wants more than a finished shell: a place to live with Camila, host friends, make music, and build a life that feels truly his.

---

## Goals

- Finish the house with Camila — primary personal goal.
- Protect the concrete future the house, marriage, and projects represent when the crisis hits.
- Early crisis priority: understand what is happening and protect the people he cares about — without necessarily knowing the best way.
