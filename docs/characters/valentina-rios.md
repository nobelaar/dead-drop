## Summary

Valentina (“Vale”) is one of [Nicolás Ferreyra](nicolas-ferreyra.md)’s best friends: independent, blunt, strong-willed. She knows what she likes, has her own opinions, and does not change decisions just to please others.

**Nico is in love with her.** That need not be stated openly. Friendship is real and independent of his feelings. She is not obligated to reciprocate; romance is not a player-choice reward.

She works as a **project manager at another company** (not Nico’s workplace). Job can stay light on-screen unless the story needs it.

## Personality

Independent, frontal, strong personality. Does not need to constantly prove she is free. Can commit to people, care about friends, accept help without feeling she loses autonomy.

Hard to convince when she thinks someone is deciding *for* her — but she can listen and change her mind when reasons feel valid.

Humor can be sharp and spontaneous. Little patience for possessiveness or people who confuse caring with a right to control someone’s life.

Not emotionally invulnerable: doubt, fear, need for support — not always knowing how to ask.

Not rebel-for-show; not cold or unreachable.

### Friendship with Nico (and romance rules)

- Shared trust, history, familiarity that need not be constantly explained.
- Nico may give her special attention, remember throwaway details, worry in ways that tip the player to his feelings.
- Vale need not be oblivious to everything — and need not know he is in love. Open.
- No obligation to reciprocate. Friendship keeps value without romance.
- Not required: confession, or her demanding he name his feelings.
- Feelings toward Nico (romantic vs friendship-only): leave open unless locked later.
- **Not** a reward for player choices.

### Autonomy (design rules)

- Does not exist to motivate Nico, spark jealousy, be rescued, or justify the campaign.
- Can refuse his help, choose paths he rejects, force him to accept he cannot decide for her.
- Independence ≠ arbitrary or emotionally sealed; choices answer to **her** motives.
