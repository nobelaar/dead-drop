## Summary

Bruno is a bricklayer with practical field and agriculture knowledge from rural origins and work experience. He knows physical labor, tools, materials, and the difficulty of repairing or building something for real.

Knowledge is **practical** — lived experience, not apocalypse prep school.

## Personality

Observant, direct, practical. Prefers an honest conversation to a long explanation that goes nowhere.

Has a sense of humor; can rib his friends hard, especially when someone overcomplicates a problem he sees as simple.

Not necessarily quiet or rough. Can talk for hours when comfortable; strong opinions on many things.

Problem-solving style: what is in front of him, what resources exist, what can actually be done. That does not mean he is always right or that he scorns academic/tech knowledge.

Especially annoyed by promises that never become actions.

**Must not** auto-default to mute tough guy, force-first conflict, or infallible nature expert.

---

## Background

- Rural background; first-hand field work. Later moved toward a more urban setting for work opportunities.
- Bricklayer: tools, materials, repairs, construction.
- Practical knowledge of crops, agricultural cycles, land care — from experience, not unlimited rural empire.
- Not a ranch owner; no infinite rural resources.
- Modest economics; used to managing income; material problems are not fixed by good intentions alone.
- Rural origin ≠ whole identity; does not imply he wants to abandon current life for “return to the land.”
- **Not established:** medical training, military experience, advanced survivalist kit.
