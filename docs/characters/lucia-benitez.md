## Summary

Lucía is a writer and one of [Nicolás Ferreyra](nicolas-ferreyra.md)’s five best friends. She especially values their philosophical conversations — exploring ideas, testing convictions, talking about what everyday life rarely holds space for.

**She is secretly in love with Nico** and has never told him. He need not know. Friendship stands on its own; she is not a backup to [Vale](valentina-rios.md).

## Personality

Reflective, observant; enjoys exploring ideas. Sensitive to contradictions between what people say and how they act.

Does **not** always understand others better than they do. Can misread a situation, get stuck in her own ideas, or use intellectual framing to avoid an uncomfortable feeling.

Has humor — often subtler than her friends’. Can enjoy absurd talk as much as a serious philosophy argument.

Not metaphor-only speech; not a deep answer for every question. Sometimes she does not know what to think — that uncertainty is part of her.

Can voice complex ideas and still struggle to name what matters most personally.

**Must not** be only mysterious muse, quote machine, or walking philosophy DLC for the protagonist.

- Writes literature; seeks her own voice. Professional situation modest and uncertain — writing matters even without external recognition or stable income from it.

### Friendship with Nico

- Core: philosophical talk (identity, freedom, morality, death, power, meaning of decisions). Often no conclusion — value in a new question.
- Mutual challenge without every disagreement becoming personal war.
- Nico can explore ideas without forcing them into immediate practical decisions; she shares questions that matter to her.
- Intellectual intimacy ≠ total knowledge of each other.

### Feelings toward Nico (design rules)

- Subtle tells OK (old remembered lines, caring about overlooked things, stretching a conversation) — compatible with real friendship; player need not clock it at once.
- No constant jealousy, obvious flirting, or attention-seeking sacrifice arcs.
- Feelings must not drive every choice or force a dramatic confession.
- Bond can change without predetermined romance ending.

## Voice / dialogue notes

## Narrative potential / crisis

Can challenge Nico’s justifications and offer other readings — **not** infallible moral compass of the story.

Secret feelings add tension; need not resolve or become romance in-campaign.
